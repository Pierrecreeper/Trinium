Trinium Clear - Trinium proprietary license

An independent Trinium color grading shader. No Iris or Sodium required.
Place this folder or its ZIP in Minecraft's shaderpacks folder. Open Video
settings > Shaders, select Trinium Clear and choose Apply.

This is a color grading example, without custom lighting, water or shadows.
The independent renderer currently supports composite/final image passes.
Complementary and other terrain/shadow shaderpacks are not supported yet.
